🎲 attested-rng

Numbers drawn inside an AMD SEV-SNP confidential VM — bound to a nonce you chose

Your browser generates a fresh 32-byte nonce for every draw. The draw's transcript folds that nonce in, so the response is bound to a value only you know — a replayed or prefabricated draw can't match it. You can recompute the numbers from the transcript right here (button below), and you can verify where they were computed with enclava verify (bottom of the page).

    What this proves — and what it doesn't.
    Attested: the draw ran inside a hardware-isolated SEV-SNP guest, under the pinned image
    and signed policy, with memory the host cannot read; your nonce makes it fresh.
    Not attested: information-theoretic randomness — the entropy source is the
    guest kernel's CSPRNG, and you are trusting the code the policy pins (read it: this
    repo). State continuity is the draw-log chain above; the platform does not by itself
    prevent volume rollback. This page is a claim; the proof is:
